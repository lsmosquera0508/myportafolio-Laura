Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Founded in 2015, AUREOLE has established itself as a beacon of gastronomic innovation in the heart of the city. Our philosophy is simple: to create extraordinary dining experiences that awaken the senses and celebrate the art of cooking.

Under the guidance of Executive Chef Marcus Sterling, our team of culinary artisans crafts each dish with precision, passion, and the finest locally-sourced ingredients. We believe that dining is not merely about sustenance—it is a celebration of life itself.

Chef Marcus Sterling

An Intimate Journey

Chef's Table Tasting Menu

Seven courses. One story. Take a seat at the chef's counter and watch Executive Chef Marcus Sterling compose each course before your eyes — an immersive dining ritual reserved for eight guests per evening.

Approximately 3 Hours 8 Guests per Seating Tuesday – Saturday

Amuse-Bouche

Golden Beet Tartlet

Crystalized beetroot, whipped chèvre and micro herbs in a buttery sablé shell.

Wine Pairing Blanc de Blancs Champagne
II
First Course

Hokkaido Scallop Crudo

Hand-dived scallop with yuzu kosho, green apple and cold-pressed olive oil pearls.

Wine Pairing Sancerre, Loire Valley 2023
III
Second Course

Wild Mushroom Velouté

Porcini cream, black truffle shavings and a delicate hazelnut praline crumble.

Wine Pairing White Burgundy, Chassagne-Montrachet

Butter-Poached Halibut
